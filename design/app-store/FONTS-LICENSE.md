# Fonts used for the App Store screenshots

Both fonts are only used to draw the App Store screenshots' headlines and sub-lines (`store-frame.mjs`); the app
itself doesn't ship them. Both are under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

- `fraunces.woff2`: Fraunces, Copyright 2020 The Fraunces Project Authors (github.com/undercasetype/Fraunces).
  Licence: [`Fraunces-LICENSE.txt`](Fraunces-LICENSE.txt).
- `instrument-sans.woff2`: Instrument Sans, Copyright 2022 The Instrument Sans Project Authors
  (github.com/Instrument/instrument-sans). Licence: [`InstrumentSans-LICENSE.txt`](InstrumentSans-LICENSE.txt).
