Widgets

On every screen, too.

Small, medium and large widgets on the Mac desktop and on iPhone, iPad and Android Home Screens, plus the iPhone Lock Screen. Each can follow your city or keep its own. See them live ›

Next 24 hours

The day, hour by hour.

A scrolling strip with a temperature line, the sky for each hour, and the chance of rain when it matters.

5-day forecast

Five days on one scale.

Lows and highs on range bars that share one labelled scale, with a dot for right now. Tap to open.

Plain words

One sentence tells you everything.

“Rain likely around 3 PM (60%).” “Warming to 68°F / 20°C by 2 PM.”

Your cities

Up to five places, plus where you are.

Search anywhere on Earth, even offline. Say yes to Use My Location and the place you’re in comes first, kept up to date as you move. Say no and it never asks again.

Privacy

No account. No tracking.

Open weather data from Open-Meteo, refreshed every 15 minutes. Free and open source, forever.

Install on a Mac.

1

Download TwoDegrees.dmg and drag Two Degrees into Applications.

2

Double-click to open. It’s signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens like any other app. Agree to the terms once, and you’re in.

3

Add your cities with the + button. The Dock and menu bar fill in on their own.

4

Want a widget? Right-click your desktop, choose Edit Widgets, and search for Two Degrees.
